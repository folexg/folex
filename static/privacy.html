Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Folex Social Downloader saves videos from links you paste and copies videos stored on your own phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell data.

What we handle

  • Links you paste. When you tap Find video or Save video, the link is sent to our server. The server contacts the website the link points to, reads the video details and prepares the file.
  • Video files from links. The server makes a temporary copy so it can send it to you, and deletes it when the transfer ends. We do not keep copies.
  • WhatsApp videos. Videos you choose or share from your phone are handled inside your browser and are not uploaded to our server.

What is stored on your device

  • App files kept by your browser so the app opens quickly and can update itself.
  • Videos shared to the app from another app are kept in temporary browser storage until you clear the list or next open the app, then removed.
  • A cookie named dlstart that holds a random number and expires within two minutes. The page uses it to know your download has started. It does not identify you.
  • No tracking cookies and no advertising identifiers.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Render, runs the server and may record standard logs, such as IP address, time, browser type and the address requested. The address requested includes the link you pasted. Retention is controlled by the host, so see Render's own privacy policy.

Third parties

The site you paste a link from receives requests from our server, not from your device. The preview image shown after you find a video loads directly from the platform that hosts it, so that platform can see your device's address when it loads. This site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove stored app files, or uninstall the app from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
